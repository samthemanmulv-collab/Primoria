# Primoria V7.2 — Study Layer

## New
- Exact text-selection highlighting inside verified primary-source readings.
- Paragraph-level saved passages.
- Paragraph-linked private notes.
- Unified Notebook for saved passages, notes, and reflections.
- Curated Ideas page connecting recurring questions across the five verified courses.
- Idea chips on course pages and inside readings.
- Saved counter now includes both saved readings and saved passages.
- V7.1 local progress remains compatible.

## Privacy
Highlights, passage notes, saved passages, bookmarks, answers, and reflections are stored in the browser's local storage only. This build does not add a server sync layer.

## Annotation behavior
Text highlights are intentionally conservative: a selected highlight must stay within one paragraph. This keeps saved quote anchors stable without introducing a complex text-position database.
