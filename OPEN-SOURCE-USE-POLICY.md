# Primoria Open-Source and Public-Domain Use Policy

1. Primoria bundles a text only after recording the exact work, translator or editor, edition, source provider, rights status, source URL, and review date.
2. A public-domain ancient original does not automatically clear a modern translation, introduction, annotation, image, or audio recording.
3. Runtime code does not scrape or bulk-download source repositories. Source acquisition is an editorial step performed before release.
4. Project Gutenberg material must retain clear provenance and a link to the applicable Project Gutenberg license. Primoria does not imply endorsement or use the Project Gutenberg trademark as part of its brand.
5. Internet Archive items are links only unless the exact item has been independently cleared. Availability on the Archive is not treated as proof of public-domain status.
6. Wikisource material may be public domain or freely licensed. The exact page license must be recorded, and attribution/share-alike requirements must be followed where applicable.
7. Perseus or other digital-library items are used only when the individual text or data object displays reuse terms compatible with the intended use.
8. External links open the provider’s own site. Primoria does not bypass access controls, lending restrictions, rate limits, robots rules, or download limits.
9. Each graded answer stores passage evidence. Reflections are not graded.
10. Works with unresolved rights remain metadata-only and may not display substantial text.
